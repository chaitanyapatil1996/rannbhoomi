On Arrival

Checking In

Every athlete checks in in person before competing — there's no online check-in step.

1

Find your zone's check-in desk

Staff at check-in will have your registration on file by name or bib number.

2

Confirm your bib and wave

Your wave is your start group for Battle 1. Check in with time to spare before it's called — don't wait for the last minute.

3

Didn't register in advance?

Walk-in spot registration is available at check-in, subject to slots being open. Speak to the staff at the desk.

The Format

Three Battles, One Day

You're eliminated at every stage — only the strongest field advances to the next Battle.

Battle 1

Qualifiers

7 stations, 2 minutes on / 30 seconds off. Max reps or distance at each station — your score is the total across all seven. Top 30 male & top 30 female advance.

Battle 2

Semi-Finals

A 20-minute AMRAP: Rowing → Devil's Press → Overhead KB Walk → Burpee Box Jump. Ranked by rounds completed. Top 10 male & top 10 female advance.

Battle 3

Finals

The workout is classified until after Battle 2 finishes on event day. Only the 20 finalists (10 male, 10 female) find out — and compete.
